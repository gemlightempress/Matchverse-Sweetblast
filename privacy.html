Privacy Policy

What Matchverse collects, why, and what choices you have about it.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Overview

Matchverse: Sweet Blast is developed and published by Gemlight Empress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Game Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain game-related information on your device, including:

  • Your Delights
  • Cocoa pods, gummy bears and honey pots you have collected
  • Boosters
  • Lives
  • Level progress and stars
  • Achievements
  • Daily reward streak
  • Lucky Spin usage
  • The display name you choose
  • Sound, music, vibration, and accessibility settings

Google Play Games Sign-In

The game signs in automatically using Google Play Games Services and the Google account already on your device. This happens silently in the background — you are not asked to choose an account, and no sign-in screen is shown.

If you sign in, Google Play Games Services provides your:

  • Player ID
  • Display name
  • Profile picture

These may appear on your profile in Settings. Signing in also allows your game progress to be backed up using Google Play Games Cloud Save.

If sign-in is not possible — there is no Google account on the device, Play Games is unavailable, or there is no connection — the game continues as a guest with your progress stored only on your device. It remains fully playable either way.

You can sign in or re-sync at any time with Sync Now in Settings. That is the only place the game may show Google’s account picker.

Purchases

In-app purchases of Delights are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google. We receive only confirmation of what was purchased.

Sharing With Friends

Settings includes an option to share your progress to Facebook, WhatsApp or Instagram. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. When you do, the game hands an image and a link to the app you picked; what happens next is governed by that app’s own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy of anything you share.

Advertising Identifiers

The game displays banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. The advertising network may collect device advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data to serve and measure advertisements, subject to your device’s advertising settings.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Save your game progress and settings on your device.
  • Back up and sync your progress through Google Play Games Cloud Save while you are signed in.
  • Show your chosen name and profile picture on your in-game profile.
  • Deliver Delights that you purchase.
  • Display and measure advertising through Google AdMob.
  • Send optional reminders, such as when your lives are full, a daily reward is ready, an achievement is ready to claim, or your Lucky Spin is waiting. These reminders are scheduled on your device and can be turned off with the Reminders switch in Settings.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in, player profile, and Cloud Save.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Google AdMob — Advertising.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability reporting.
  • Google Play In-App Updates — offering you a newer version of the game when one is published.

Crash Reporting

When the game stops unexpectedly, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report so we can fix the problem. A report describes the crash itself and the device it happened on: the model, the version of Android, how much memory was free, the language and region setting, and where in the game’s code the failure occurred. It also includes a random installation identifier that lets us tell one device’s reports apart from another’s.

Crash reports do not contain your name, your email address, your Google account, your game progress, or anything you have typed into the game. We use them only to diagnose faults and improve stability, and Google deletes them on its own schedule (90 days for most report data).

Children’s Privacy

The game is designed to be suitable for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required for the game to function, such as advertising, which is configured to respect applicable child-directed treatment settings when the app is listed for a young audience.

Cloud Save & Progress Sync

While you are signed in, your progress is saved on your device and backed up to Google Play Games Cloud Save automatically.

Playing on another device with the same Google account can restore your progress there. When progress exists on multiple devices, the device with the more advanced save is kept.

If you play offline, your progress continues to be saved on your device and will sync the next time you are online. You can also manually initiate synchronization by tapping “Sync Now” in Settings.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can erase your game progress at any time by using “Reset Progress” in Settings.

This clears the progress stored on your device and, if you are signed in, replaces your Cloud Save with a fresh start.

You can also clear the game’s data through your device settings or uninstall the game.

Any data held by Google in connection with your Google account is retained and deleted according to your Google Account settings.

Your Choices

You have the following choices:

  • You can play without signing in by choosing “Continue without signing in.”
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device’s advertising settings.
  • You can turn off sound, music, vibration, and reminders in Settings.
  • You can change your display name at any time from your profile in Settings.
  • You can erase your game progress at any time using “Reset Progress” in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game or applicable law.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date above and ask you to review the updated policy in the game.
